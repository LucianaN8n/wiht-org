About WIHT

A private credentialing
body built for integrity

We are transparent about what our credentials represent — and what they do not. That clarity protects our members and the people they serve.

What WIHT Credentials Authorize

WIHT-certified practitioners are qualified to offer holistic therapy services, including behavioral coaching, integrative wellness sessions, complementary therapeutic techniques, and neuroscience-informed support.

Our credentials are recognized by partner institutions in the Americas and Europe, and are designed to meet the standards expected by clients, wellness centers, and private practices worldwide.

Scope of Practice

WIHT credentials are awarded by a private credentialing organization and do not constitute a license to practice medicine, clinical psychology, or any government-regulated healthcare profession.

Practitioners should accurately represent their qualifications in all markets. In Brazil, clinical diagnosis is reserved for licensed psychologists (CRP). In the US, regulated titles such as "Licensed Psychologist" or "LCSW" require state licensure.

Legal Disclaimer: Saber Consciente Worldwide LLC is a private credentialing and membership organization incorporated under the laws of the State of Wyoming, USA. Membership and certification issued by WIHT do not constitute a license to practice medicine, psychology, counseling, or any regulated healthcare profession in any jurisdiction. All practitioners listed in the WIHT directory are independently responsible for complying with the professional regulations applicable in their country, state, or region of practice. WIHT credentials are complementary professional designations intended to recognize training in holistic and integrative wellness modalities.
