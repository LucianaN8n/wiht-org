FAQ

Common questions

Is the WIHT credential recognized internationally?

Yes. WIHT is a private credentialing body incorporated in Wyoming, USA, and our credentials are recognized by partner institutions across the Americas and Europe. The Senior International Holistic Therapist (SIHT) credential is accepted by complementary therapy associations in multiple countries, including those in Brazil, the US, Portugal, and Argentina.

Can I practice in the United States with this credential?

Yes, as a holistic health practitioner or behavioral wellness coach — titles that are widely used and not regulated by the federal government in most US states. You may not use regulated titles such as Licensed Psychologist, LPC, or LCSW without the applicable state license. Many WIHT members practice online internationally, which opens access to the large Brazilian community in the US.

Does this credential allow clinical diagnosis?

No. WIHT credentials do not authorize clinical diagnosis in any country. Clinical diagnosis is a function reserved for licensed healthcare professionals (psychologists, physicians, or psychiatrists) under the laws of most jurisdictions. WIHT practitioners offer complementary therapeutic support, coaching, and integrative wellness services.

How long does the certification process take?

Once you complete your course and submit your application, the review process takes up to 15 business days. Your digital certificate and directory profile are activated simultaneously.

Are the courses in Portuguese?

Yes. All affiliated courses are taught in Brazilian Portuguese and hosted on the Hotmart platform. The WIHT credential itself is issued in English and Portuguese, making it suitable for international use.
